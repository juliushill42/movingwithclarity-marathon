Here's what's happening behind the screen.

The website starts with example messages that people have labeled “needs attention” or “can wait.” A small machine-learning algorithm counts word patterns in each group. When you type something new, it compares the words and makes a guess. When you correct it, your example becomes part of its next lesson.

That's real mathematical pattern learning, not a remote ChatGPT call. It can be wrong, and it doesn't understand the message the way a person does. The lesson stays inside this browser tab.
